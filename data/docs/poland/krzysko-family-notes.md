# Krzysko / Kryzsko family notes (Polish line, Patrick's direct paternal line)

Line: Patrick → Francis Duane Kryzsko (1935–2017) → Alfred James Krzysko (1907–1995) →
Frank Sylvester Kryzsko (1882–1966) → Andrew (Joseph Andrew) Krzysko/Krzyska.

## From Jim Kulas's 2008 article [1]

- **Andrew** (also "Joseph" in records) **Krzyska** was born in Poznań (Posen, then Prussia).
  His parents were **Joseph Krysko/Krzysko** (born about 1820) and **Agnes Mastek** (born about 1820).
  The article lists eight children of Andrew; the sixth, Andrew Joe, was born in Detroit.
- **Spelling:** when Frank's children started public school at Greenbush, Minnesota, their teacher,
  Ms. Thompson, changed the name from Krzyska to **Kryzsko**. Andrew's second son, **John Anton**,
  became **Crystal**: a Grand Forks grocery clerk heard "Krzysko" and wrote "Crystal".
- **Frank Kryzsko** married **Katherine Marciniak** on 16 Feb 1903. They had six children,
  lived near Greenbush (Roseau County), then East Grand Forks, and from November 1918 farmed
  south of Drayton, ND.
- **Clara Maria Kryzsko** (17 Jan 1905 – 30 Oct 2002), baptised at St. Aloysius, Leo, MN.
  She married (1) W. Schieller on 14 Nov 1922 (he died 1932) and (2) **Ludwik Marion Kulas**,
  a widower, on 3 Jul 1937 at St. Stanislaus, Warsaw, ND. She is buried at Sacred Heart, Minto, ND.
  Jim credits her with gathering most of the data for the article.

## Sylvester J. "Kris" Kryzsko (1903–1991), great-uncle; Kryzsko Commons

- Born 3 Dec 1903 (St. Aloysius, Leo, Roseau County, MN); died 20 Jan 1991, Winona, MN [1][2].
- Married LaVone Bradley (1909–1995) on 6 May 1934 at St. Thomas Pro-Cathedral, Winona [1].
- Banker: first president of Town and Country State Bank, later president of Winona National &
  Savings Bank [2].
- Resident director of Winona State, 1951–1965; on the Minnesota State College Board 1951–1965,
  president 1957–1961; drove the revenue-bond program that financed residence halls and student
  centers across the state colleges; chairman of the Development Council for Minnesota State
  Colleges from 1968 [1][2][3].
- **Kryzsko Commons**, the student union at Winona State University, is named in his honor [1][2][3].
- Gave Jim the information on the Krzysko name change [1].

## Conflicts to resolve

- Andrew's parents: the article has Joseph Krzysko and Agnes Mastek, both born about 1820. The
  GEDCOM has Józef Krzyśko (1804–1856) and Agnieszka Maślek (1811–1870), and Andrew as
  Joseph Andrew Krzysko (1843–1930).
- Alfred's middle name: "John" in the article's table, "James" in the GEDCOM.
