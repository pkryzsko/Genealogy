## 1. Rules (read first)

- **Trust levels** (`data/README.md`, `data/manifest.yaml`): `source` > `draft` > `unverified` >
  `deprecated`. Anything not listed in the manifest counts as `unverified`. If the manifest and
  the frontmatter disagree, the lower level applies. Material from GEDCOM/Geni is `unverified`.
- Never add living people here.
- Family tradition stays labelled as tradition.
- Every new file under `data/` needs a manifest entry.

## 3. Family lines

- **Norway:** Peter Pettersen Lerretsdalen, later Peter Sorlien (1888–1966), born at Lerretsdalen
  under Gjevle, Nordre Land. His father was Petter Pedersen, "Petter Leresdal" (1849–1925), a
  crofter. The place name also appears as Leresdal(en), Linedsdalen and Lenæsdalen, and a modern
  road sign reads "Lerretsdalsvegen". Family tradition says the name comes from an early owner
  called Lars (unverified).
- **Ireland:** James Patrick Murphy (1854–1942) married Anne Sherlock (1856–1920) on
  2 May 1876 at St. Peter Celestine, Pakenham, Ontario. Fr. Dominic Lavin, a survivor of the
  1847 famine emigration from Sligo, officiated. Arthur Murphy came from Enniscorthy, Wexford.
  The family later farmed in Ferry Township (Manvel, ND).
- **Poland:** Frank Sylvester Kryzsko (1882–1966) and Katherine Marciniak (1884–1948) moved from
  Greenbush, MN, where a teacher altered the spelling of the name, to a farm south of Drayton, ND.
  Alfred James Kryzsko (1907–1995) and Margaret Evelyn Murphy (1915–1986) lived at
  1832 Riverside Drive, Grand Forks.
