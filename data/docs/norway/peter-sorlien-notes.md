# Peter Sorlien (1888–1966)

The GEDCOM records him as **Peter Pettersen Lerretsdalen**, also known as **Peter Sorlien**
(nickname *Sørlien*): born 31 January 1888 at Lerretsdalen under Gjevle (Nordre Land, Norway),
baptised 11 March 1888, died 25 January 1966. His Geni "about" note cites
Nordre Land prestekontor, Ministerialbok nr. 3, 1882–1896, p. 16 #3
(https://media.digitalarkivet.no/view/6128/31552/17). Check that page to promote him to `source`.

- Father of **Edna Allenora (Eleanore) Sorlien Schultz**, the anchor of the Norwegian line,
  with his wife **Gina Oline Erikson** (1893–1969). Patrick → mother → Eleanore → Peter.
  [Geni GEDCOM export (26 Jul 2026)]
- Appears on Geni with four photos uploaded by Patrick Francis Kryzsko (albums "Sorlien" and
  "Patrick Francis Kryzsko's photos").
- Tagged in the group photo **"Landings Lag Stevne, Hillsboro, ND, June 20–21, 1952"**
  (Geni photo #77), a reunion of a Norwegian *bygdelag*. People in the photo are numbered;
  the numbers are not legible at this resolution.
