# Norwegian line

Anchor: **Edna Allenora Sorlien** (20 Nov 1917 – 26 Jun 1992), Patrick Kryzsko's maternal
grandmother. Edna Allenora is her legal name. She disliked "Edna" and went by **Eleanore**
(Eleanore Schultz after marrying Lawrence John Schultz). The GEDCOM lists both names, and
Find a Grave memorial 128429577 is under "Eleanore Schultz".

Scope: Eleanore, all of her recorded ancestors, and her own siblings (no spouses unless they
are also ancestors, and no siblings' descendants). Generated by
`tools/geni_import.py run --gedcom ... --line norway` from the Geni GEDCOM export (26 Jul 2026).

- `index.json`: everyone in the line, with relation and generation.
- `profiles/`: one JSON file per person.
- `tree.mmd`: Mermaid ancestor diagram.
- `peter-sorlien-notes.md`: notes on her father.

Everything in the line is deceased in the GEDCOM, so the public and private copies are
identical. The export holds 5,000 people (its upper limit), so deeper generations may be
missing. Re-run the import when there's a newer or larger export. All profiles are
`unverified` until checked against church books (Digitalarkivet) or other records.
