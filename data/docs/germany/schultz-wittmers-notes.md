# Schultz and Wittmers (German-related branch)

From the Geni GEDCOM export (26 Jul 2026), Find a Grave links recorded there, and Patrick Kryzsko. Deceased people only;
living relatives are in the private repository.

- **Lawrence John Schultz** (1913–1974): Patrick's maternal grandfather; husband of
  Eleanore (Edna Allenora Sorlien) Schultz of the Norwegian line.
- **Katherine (Catherine) Bielefeld Schultz** (19 Apr 1884 – 25 Mar 1954): Lawrence's mother.
  Find a Grave 24858352.
- **Ludowica Bielefeld Wittmers** (born 1892/93, died Dec 1962 or 19 Dec 1963; the GEDCOM
  gives both): Josef Wittmers' mother. Find a Grave 252557400.
- Katherine and Ludowica were sisters (same parents in the GEDCOM), so Lawrence Schultz and
  Josef Wittmers were first cousins. This matches what Patrick was told.
- **Josef Henry Wittmers** (1924–2007) and his wife **Elfriede Hedwig Löwendey** (1928–2005),
  both German.

## 1952 immigration

The INS Manifest of In-Bound Passengers (Aliens), manifest no. 43, S.S. *America*, cabin class,
lists **Josef Wittmers** (28, married, German, travel doc. I-1381197) and **Elfriede Wittmers**
(23, married, German, I-1381198), sailing from Bremerhaven 21 July 1952 and arriving at
New York 29 July 1952, destination "c/o L. Schultz, Buxton Township, North Dakota", with
5 pieces of baggage. Patrick says L. Schultz is Lawrence, and that Lawrence and Eleanore
sponsored them.

The manifest image is in the private repository (the page also lists unrelated passengers).
